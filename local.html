Cultural Tips & Gestures to Avoid

  • 🙏 Always greet with “Namaste” using both hands.
  • 🚫 Avoid touching someone with your feet.
  • 🧥 Dress modestly in temples and rural areas.
  • 🫱 Use right hand only for eating and giving items.
